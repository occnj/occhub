What is inside
START HERE.html — open it in a browser: every section and item, with its text and the pictures and documents attached to it.
One folder per section (Sermon Notes, Events, Leadership, Volunteer Newsletter…), with one folder per item: a text.txt and that item's own pictures and files. Gallery photos, team members and Next Steps sit in their parent's folder.
Not used by anything/ — uploads no item refers to any more.
Spreadsheets/ — every table as a .csv for Excel or Google Sheets.
Database/oasis.db — the complete database, to restore the Hub.
To add it to another Hub — every item folder has an item.json (its database record, which field points at which file, where each file goes: static/uploads/…, and its page routes), all of it in manifest.json, plus HOW TO IMPORT.md and Restore/restore_to_hub.py. Give the unzipped folder to Claude Code, or run: 
python3 Restore/restore_to_hub.py /home/occnj/occ_hub --merge --dry-run
It contains people's names, emails, phone numbers and prayer requests. Keep it private. Passwords are never included.
